How to read this

The dates, places and scholars are from the published record. The pairing of each one with a phone habit is the experiment. Nobody can show that a hand stencil and a selfie come from the same impulse. They are placed side by side so you can decide.

The cosmic year is Carl Sagan's calendar, updated to today's figure for the age of the universe. Sagan used 15 billion years. Here 13.8 billion years are mapped onto 365 days, so one second stands for about 438 years.
