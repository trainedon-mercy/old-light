# Old Light

An experiment in online culture, anthropology and deep time.

**Live:** https://trainedon-mercy.github.io/old-light/

Pick something you did online today: posted a selfie, joined a pile-on, doomscrolled. You get three things back:

- the oldest version of it we have evidence for, such as a hand stencil on a cave wall in Sulawesi from at least 67,800 years ago
- the name a scholar gave the behaviour
- where it lands if the whole history of the universe is squeezed into one year, and how far light from that moment has travelled since

A timeline down the side runs from today to the Big Bang on a log scale and marks the one you picked.

## How to read it

The dates, places and scholars are from the published record, and were checked against sources in October 2026. The pairing of each one with a phone habit is the experiment. Nobody can show that a hand stencil and a selfie come from the same impulse. They are placed side by side so you can decide.

Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. One file, no build step.
